KYC — Verify Your Identity

Required before your first withdrawal. All documents are encrypted and stored securely.

📋 Why KYC?

In compliance with international AML regulations, all users must verify their identity before withdrawing funds. This protects you and the platform from fraud.

1. Government ID (Required)

Upload a clear photo of your passport, national ID, or driver's license.

2. Payment Card (Required)

Upload a photo of the card used for your deposit. You may cover the middle 8 digits.

3. Selfie with ID (Required)

Hold your ID next to your face and take a selfie.

Verification typically takes 24-48 hours. You will be notified by email.
